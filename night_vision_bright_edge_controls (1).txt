NIGHT VISION MONOCULAR - CONTROLS
LAFVIN ESP32-S3 1.69" LCD board (OV3660 camera)
Sketch: lafvin_nightvision_nolib.ino

SETTINGS (14 in GRAYSCALE, 14 in COLOUR)
  L1 - L4 : low-gain settings for bright or supplemental IR
            (L1 is the dimmest, for when the picture is washed out)
  1 - 10  : night gain levels, brighter and grainier as the number rises
            (10 = maximum gain, the old BOOST setting)
  After the last setting the cycle repeats all 14 with EDGE HIGHLIGHTING
  (thick yellow outlines drawn on the picture), then returns to plain.

BOOT BUTTON (the GPIO0 button)
  Short press (under 0.8 s) : next step
                                L1..L4, 1..10, then the same again with
                                edges, then plain again, and so on
                                (the camera restarts briefly at each wrap)
  Medium hold (0.8 - 2 s)   : change screen brightness
                                100% -> 50% -> 15% -> 4% -> back to 100%
  Long hold (over 2 s)      : switch between GRAYSCALE and COLOUR
  (The action happens when you release the button.)

STATUS STRIP (top of the screen)
  Square       : grey = grayscale, orange = colour
  First number : current setting, L1-L4 or 1-10 (cyan);
                 a yellow E after it = edges on
  Second number: frames per second (green)
  Four bars    : screen brightness (4 lit = 100%, 1 lit = 4%)

RESET BUTTON
  Restarts the program.

TIPS
  - Washed out by IR? Step down to the L settings. Too dark? Step up.
  - Edge highlighting costs some frame rate. In the sketch, EDGE_THRESHOLD
    sets sensitivity (lower = more edges, more speckle) and EDGE_THICK
    sets thickness (1 = thick, 0 = thin).
  - More IR light is better than more gain.
  - Lower screen brightness keeps your eye dark-adapted and saves battery.
  - Do not hold BOOT while powering on. That puts the board into
    firmware download mode and nothing will run.

STATUS LED
  The RGB LED (GPIO 48, from Lafvin's pin_config.h) is switched off at startup.
  Set LED_GPIO to -1 near the top of the sketch to leave it alone.

STARTUP ERROR SCREENS
  Solid RED screen     : PSRAM not found (set PSRAM to OPI PSRAM)
  Solid MAGENTA screen : camera failed to start (check the camera ribbon cable)

FLASHING NEW CODE
  1. Hold BOOT, plug in the USB cable, release BOOT.
  2. Upload.
  3. Press RESET when it finishes.
